# Candidate reviews — head-to-head judging

Judged against the kit's published criteria, most important first:
1. One-star review of an everyday essential, deadpan, unmistakably about the thing itself.
2. Sharpest true grievance — the small daily annoyance everyone recognises but has never seen written down.
3. Holds the deadpan: no winking, no explaining the joke, no exclamation marks.
4. Turns in the last few words, so the sting arrives after the complaint has been read straight.
5. Tight and specific: one joke told cleanly, no filler, no second joke crowding the first.
(Length for its own sake does not count; on an exact rate tie the smaller text wins.)

## Candidates

- **A — gravity (157 chars):** "One star. Gravity has held me down my entire life, never once taken a day off, and still expects me to carry the groceries up three flights of stairs myself."
- **B — sleep (153 chars):** "One star. Sleep demands eight hours every single night, offers no refund for the bad dreams, and leaves at 3 a.m. to let me stare at the ceiling instead."
- **C — sun (157 chars):** "One star. The sun shows up uninvited every morning, bleaches the curtains, fades the sofa, and has the nerve to set at 4 p.m. in winter when I still need it."
- **D — Mondays (166 chars):** OVER the 160-char cap — eliminated on the gate alone.
- **E — oxygen (147 chars):** "One star. Oxygen is free, everywhere, and completely non-negotiable, yet somehow my lungs still run out of it halfway up a single flight of stairs."
- **F — Wi-Fi (158 chars):** "One star. The Wi-Fi works perfectly until the one video call that matters, then freezes my face mid-sentence and blames the router, which is in the next room."

## Verdicts

- **E beats B:** both hold the deadpan and turn late, but E's grievance (out of breath on one flight of stairs) is sharper and less joked-about than 3 a.m. insomnia; B's "bad dreams" line is a second mini-grievance crowding the joke.
- **E beats A:** A's turn lands mid-sentence and its grievance is really about stairs, not gravity; E's sting is the final clause and the grievance belongs to oxygen itself.
- **E beats C:** C carries two grievances (bleaching + early sunset) — two jokes, fails criterion 5; "has the nerve" also winks.
- **E beats F:** F's turn is strong, but "blames the router" anthropomorphises (a wink), and Wi-Fi is the weakest "essential" of the set next to the kit's own examples.
- **E beats D:** D is over cap, and "act surprised" winks.

## Winner: E — oxygen (147 chars)

Strongest on criteria 2 and 4: the sharpest, most specific true grievance, with the sting arriving in the last few words. Runner-up: B — sleep.
